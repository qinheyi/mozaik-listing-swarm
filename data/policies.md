# Marketplace Listing Policy (excerpt used by the Compliance agent)

## P1 Unverifiable superlatives
Do not use unverifiable superlatives or rankings such as "best", "#1", "number one", "top rated", "world's finest" unless backed by a cited, current third-party source. Prefer specific, measurable facts (e.g. "18 bar pressure").

## P2 Medical and health claims
Do not claim that a product cures, treats, prevents or eliminates any medical condition (e.g. "cures blisters", "eliminates eye strain"). Comfort language such as "helps reduce friction" or "comfortable for long reading sessions" is allowed.

## P3 Absolute guarantees
Avoid absolute guarantees such as "100%", "never", "guaranteed", "forever" for performance properties (leaks, odor, durability). Use qualified wording such as "designed to resist leaks when the gasket is seated".

## P4 Competitor references
Do not mention competitor brands or products in titles, bullets or descriptions (e.g. BenQ, Darn Tough, Wacaco). Compatibility statements for standards (e.g. "NS-compatible capsules") are allowed.

## P5 Title format
Title must be at most 150 characters, start with the brand, contain no promotional text ("sale", "free shipping", "deal", "cheap") and no ALL CAPS words longer than 4 letters.

## P6 Bullet points
Provide 3 to 5 bullet points, each at most 220 characters. Each bullet should lead with a benefit and be backed by a spec.

## P7 Description
Description must be 300 to 1500 characters, must not contain prices, URLs, emails or phone numbers.

## P8 Honesty about limitations
If customer reviews reveal a common limitation (e.g. small tank, sizing runs large, requires 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi), the listing should set expectations honestly, typically in the description or a bullet.

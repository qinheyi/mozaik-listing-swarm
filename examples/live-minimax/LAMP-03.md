The Lumen Pro Smart LED Desk Lamp is a dimmable, adjustable LED desk lamp built for home offices and creative work. It delivers up to 1000 lumens across 5 brightness levels and 5 color temperatures (2700K-6500K), with CRI 95+ for color-accurate output that reviewers particularly recommend for photo editing. Flicker-free DC dimming means no visible flicker when you film or shoot stills on camera.

The base integrates a 15W Qi wireless charging pad plus a USB-C port, so your phone can charge while you work without a separate charger. A sturdy 3-joint arm and 180-degree rotating head let you direct light onto your keyboard, book, or model. Control it from the touch panel, the app, or by voice through Alexa and Google Home - one user schedules a warm evening mode after 9pm with a Google Home routine. Backed by a 3-year warranty.

A few things to know before you buy: the smart features require a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, and app setup may need a couple of tries on a 5 GHz-only router. The touch panel is quite sensitive, so brushing the base can change brightness unexpectedly - the app or voice control are good alternatives. The base is also fairly large and takes up noticeable desk space, so measure your workspace. Eye comfort varies by person and setup; most buyers simply like the brightness and color range.

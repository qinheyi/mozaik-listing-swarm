A 3-pack of merino wool hiking socks built for multi-day treks, winter walking and campsite use. The blend is 68% merino wool with 29% nylon and 3% elastane, and a reviewer who hiked four days on the Via Dinarica without washing reported the socks stayed fresh. Medium cushioning sits under the foot, heel and toe, which one buyer praised as perfect for long descents with no hot spots, and a built-in arch support band keeps the sock from sliding down. The flat-knit toe seam helps reduce blister risk (it is a friction-reducing seam, not a treatment for blisters), and heel and toe areas are reinforced — though see the caveat below. Each pack holds three pairs in charcoal, olive and navy at a 23 cm crew height; machine wash warm and tumble dry low.

What to expect: sizing runs large, and an EU 42 wearer found M slightly long at the toe, so consider sizing down. Toe durability is limited — one pair developed a toe hole after 3 months of daily wear (it was replaced under warranty), so the reinforcement should not be read as wear-proof. High heat drying causes shrinkage, so tumble dry low only. The 23 cm crew and merino blend can feel too warm outside cool conditions. Feedback is still thin, with 6 customer reviews averaging 3.83, so treat it as a small sample rather than broad consensus.

Nomad Brew Co.'s portable espresso maker gives you a real shot wherever you are. A manual piston drives up to 18 bar of pressure through a stainless steel brew chamber, and reviewers report rich espresso with genuine crema once you get the pumping rhythm down (roughly 15 pumps) and use a fine grind.

Because it needs no batteries or electricity, it works equally well on a campsite, in a hotel, or at your desk. At 390 g it fits in a backpack side pocket, and the included travel pouch keeps it protected in transit.

Brew with your own ground coffee or Nespresso-style capsules — the capsule adapter, dosing scoop and cleaning brush are all in the box, and cleanup is fast.

What to know before you buy: the 80 ml tank holds one shot at a time, so refilling is needed for back-to-back drinks. It does not heat water, so bring water you've already heated. Grind size guidance is light, so expect a short learning curve, and the capsule adapter can drip in the first week until the gasket is seated properly. It is a manual alternative to a moka pot, not an electric machine.
